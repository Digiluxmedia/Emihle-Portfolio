Who I am

Designing for meaningful business growth.

I’m Emihle Mkabile, founder of DigiLuxe Media. My work is grounded in the belief that strong businesses need more than visibility: they need clear positioning, intentional systems, relevant digital tools and consistent execution.

I work across business development, strategic branding, digital marketing, website and platform solutions, and opportunities to use AI and automation to improve how businesses operate.

My experience in event hosting, programme direction and workshop facilitation also gives me a people-centred perspective on communication, stakeholder engagement and bringing ideas to life.

“Ideas are powerful. Clear strategy and consistent execution are what turn them into progress.”
